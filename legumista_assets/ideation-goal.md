Propose concrete, original research ideas that exploit the discovered corpus.

Every idea must:

- **Be grounded in specific corpus papers** — cite their DOIs, and name the concrete
  finding, dataset, method, or gap each one contributes.
- **Be feasible** — rely on resources that demonstrably exist; where relevant, verify data
  or tool availability before proposing.
- **Go beyond what the corpus already establishes** — prefer ideas that fill a concrete
  gap, resolve a contradiction between studies, or transfer a proven method to an open
  question, over ideas that restate known results.

Favour precision over breadth: a few well-specified, corpus-grounded ideas beat many vague
ones.
