# Ideation goal — Lupinus genomics (Phase 3 framework)

Propose **purely computational / bioinformatic** research ideas that exploit the
discovered *Lupinus* (Fabaceae) genomics corpus — no wet-lab work and no new data
generation. Every idea must be grounded in specific corpus papers (cite their DOIs) and,
where relevant, in public data whose existence you have verified with the tools
(`ncbi_assembly_status`, `sra_runs`).

Favour ideas that:

- **Reuse existing public genomes / sequencing data** — e.g. a comparative or pangenome
  analysis across the published *Lupinus* reference assemblies, or reanalysis of
  deposited SRA reads for a species that lacks a formal assembly.
- **Connect a method proven in one legume/plant clade to an open question in _Lupinus_**
  (synteny, whole-genome-duplication history, QTL→gene resolution, alkaloid or
  disease-resistance loci).
- **Fill a concrete gap named in the corpus** (a missing genome, an under-analysed trait,
  a contradiction between studies) rather than restating what is already known.

Each idea should name the concrete datasets, tools, and corpus findings it builds on, and
be specific enough that a computational biologist could scope it into a project.
